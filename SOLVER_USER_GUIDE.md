# Solver User Guide

## Solve

Open Solver, enter a supported expression with the math keyboard, and choose
Solve, Simplify, or Check. Review interpretation, assumptions, method, steps,
rules, visual explanation, verification, and final answer as available.

## Learn Without Revealing Everything

- **Hint only** starts with the problem type and progresses toward one next step.
- **Ask me the next step** lets you enter a proposed mathematical line. Equivalent
  methods are accepted; invalid steps receive a specific explanation.
- **Practise similar** creates and verifies a local practice problem before showing it.

## Calculators

Open Calculators and search by title or group. Choosing a calculator inserts its
tested starter expression into the same Solver input. There are no separate,
inconsistent calculator engines.

## Learning Estimate

Open Learning to view local attempts, accuracy, hints, and skills for review. The
percentage is an approximate recent-performance estimate, not a formal assessment.
Use Clear learning data to remove the Solver aggregate.

## Privacy and Offline Use

Solver calculations and learning data stay on the device. Solver needs no
internet service to solve, explain, visualise, verify, or generate practice.
