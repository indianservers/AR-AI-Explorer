AR dedicated workspaces and hand interaction — 2026-10-03

Implemented
- Dedicated AR 2D Graph, AR 3D Graph, AR 2D Geometry and AR 3D Geometry routes in AR Lab.
- All four routes use the shared ARCore runtime, surface/anchor engine, canonical scene and hand controller.
- Optional Hands control: choose Move, Rotate or Scale, hold thumb/index pinch to select an object,
  then move the pinch; release commits. Two pinches support spreading and turning in the chosen tool.
- Geometry transforms edit the linked shape/solid through existing command history; graph transforms
  change its spatial placement. Cancel restores the baseline; release records one undoable gesture.
- Selection highlights and a hand cursor provide feedback. Hide controls / Show AR controls clear
  the interaction area. Placement taps remain available before anchoring, including with Hands enabled.
- Locked or hidden objects cannot be grabbed. HUD pinches cannot select objects underneath controls.
- Stable pinch delay, hysteresis, stale-frame cancellation, tracking-loss cancellation, viewport-change
  cancellation and duplicate hand-identity rejection prevent unstable updates.
- Delete/Clear cancel active gestures before modifying the construction.
- Fixed anchored rendering/picking ignoring authored translation when a native anchor exists.
  Anchor refinements now preserve the user's movement offset.

ARCore / inference integration
ARCore supplies camera/environment tracking, CPU camera images and surface anchors. Its Android API
does not provide hand landmarks. MediaPipe Tasks Vision 1.0.0 supplies on-device hand inference.
The official Hand Landmarker float16 model v1 is bundled; no inference upload or second camera session.
Native camera images are closed after copying YUV planes. One worker accepts one frame at a time,
with renderer sampling capped at 10 Hz and inference images bounded to a 480-pixel maximum dimension.
Camera rotation/crop transforms align landmarks to the rendered viewport. Gestures use physical
viewport aspect when measuring pinch and two-hand angles.

Validation actually completed
- Debug app and instrumentation APK builds: PASS.
- AR-engine unit tests: 165 passed, zero failed (19 new gesture/camera tests).
- Relevant workspace/navigation/renderer tests: 63 passed, zero failed (4 new route/transform tests).
- Emulator Android instrumentation: 1 passed. Real bundled model recognized two hands, 21 landmarks
  each, from the official right_hands.jpg fixture converted to YUV camera-plane format.
- Direct ADB UI checks on emulator-5556: opened all four dedicated routes, enabled Hands on each,
  selected Rotate and Scale in AR 2D Graph, disabled Hands again, hid controls and restored them.
  Captured route screenshots/XML. AR 2D Graph capture was refreshed using the final installed APK;
  the other three route captures precede the final help-text and Hide-controls additions.
- Corrected an existing schema migration test fixture: it hard-coded schema 10 although the writer
  has advanced. The fixture now substitutes the actual header version when constructing schema 6.

Not validated on physical hardware
No OnePlus Nord CE 5 is connected. Emulator ARCore cannot initialize its rear camera, so actual
floor/wall detection, anchored camera-frame hand interaction, occlusion, latency and device thermal
behavior remain unverified. The inference fixture and emulator control checks do not establish that
real-world AR manipulation works. The prior 100 physical surface cases remain pending.

Physical-device acceptance steps (pending)
1. Launch each of the four dedicated routes on the OnePlus, place on a floor/table, then on a wall.
2. Add y^2 / an implicit circle, a 3D surface, a triangle and a solid through the existing math UI.
3. Enable Hands, hide controls, pinch the visible object for at least 120 ms and move it.
4. Release, then Undo and Redo; confirm exactly one gesture is recorded and source geometry stays linked.
5. Choose Rotate and turn two pinches; choose Scale and spread two pinches. Verify one-hand alternatives.
6. Open the pinch, cover the camera, leave frame, switch tools, rotate the phone and background/resume.
   Verify release commits and tracking/viewport loss cancels without a jump or a stuck gesture.
7. Delete/Clear during a grab, test locked/hidden objects and pinch over HUD controls.
8. Run a 10-minute session and check latency, camera continuity, battery/thermal behavior and crashes.
